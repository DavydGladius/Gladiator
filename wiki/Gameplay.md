# Gameplay Overview

## Core loop
1. Start in the arena.
2. Fight enemy waves.
3. Enter the safe area between waves to access the shop and inventory.
4. Upgrade the player and weapons.
5. Survive as long as possible as difficulty ramps up.

## Player goals
- Stay alive by managing spacing, timing, and weapon choice.
- Build a reliable upgrade path through the shop.
- Push deeper into higher wave tiers.

## Win/lose
- The game is survival-focused. There is no hard win state in the current design.
- If the player dies, the Death Screen allows a continue option or exit to desktop.

## Difficulty scaling
- Waves increase in size and enemy stats scale with wave level.
- Every 5th wave spawns mini-boss enemies.
- Enemy speed, health, and damage ramp up over time.

## Economy and upgrades
- Enemies drop coins on death.
- Coins are spent in the shop for weapons, upgrades, and special items.
- Weapon upgrades require the matching weapon to be equipped.

## Combat pacing
- Sword provides fast close-range bursts.
- Bow enables safe ranged damage.
- Bombs provide area control but are limited by ammo.
