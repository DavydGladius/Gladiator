## At a glance
- Genre: top-down 2D action survival with roguelite-style progression.
- Core loop: fight waves, enter the safe area, shop, upgrade, repeat.
- Tech: Godot 4.x with GDScript.

## Core pillars
- Movement and spacing: dodging and positioning matter more than complex combos.
- Readable combat: weapon arcs, projectiles, and hazards are designed to be clear.
- Steady progression: upgrades and weapons keep difficulty and power scaling aligned.

## Systems map
- Combat: sword, bow, bombs, and damage multipliers.
- Economy: coins drop from enemies and fuel upgrades.
- Waves: timed spawns, grace periods, and mini-boss milestones.
- UI: wave bar, inventory, shop, pause, and death screen.

## Platforms
- Windows release builds are provided on GitHub Releases.
- The project is built in Godot 4.x and scripted in GDScript.
